Parameter-efficient fine-tuning of T5 with task-specific adapters for Indonesian abstractive summarization of news articles, aimed at keeping compute low without losing semantic fidelity.

Used a 1.99GB open-source Indonesian news dataset from Hugging Face, learned to use wandb, and achieved BERTScore F1 of ~0.80.
